# TikTok Manager 10K Welcome to TikTok Manager 10K. TikTok Manager 10K is a web application designed to help users manage their own TikTok accounts and view their published video statistics in one place. ### Features - Manage your TikTok accounts. - View your published videos. - Track available video statistics. - Access account information with user authorization. ### Privacy and Security We respect your privacy. Account information is accessed only with your authorization.
